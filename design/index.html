CaptainMorgue

Where incidents go to die. A wall-screen interface for many responders, many agents and one shared record.

Mortuary steel. Cold green-grey neutrals, hairline rules and squared corners, built dark-first for a big screen in a dim room.

Status by shape and hue. Six states, each with its own glyph, so a red-green split or a washed-out projector still reads.

One warm object. Manila is reserved for the toe tag: a part, or the whole incident, gets one only when it is dead.

Principles

Five rules every screen is checked against.

  1. 1
    Readable from the back of the room.Hierarchy by size and weight first. The clock, the severity and what is still open win every screen.
  2. 2
    Every line has a person and a time.Actor first, mono timestamp always. Nothing appears in the log without someone behind it.
  3. 3
    Motion only says something changed.New rows arrive once and settle. The live dot is the only thing that loops.
  4. 4
    Always show the why.Routing, conflicts and votes carry their reason inline, so people can trust them and correct them.
  5. 5
    The toe tag is earned.Manila is the only decorative color. It appears when a part is resolved and nowhere else.

Presses scale to 0.97. Entrances ease out under 300ms, exits are faster than entrances. Hover is color only and gated to fine pointers. The spring is reserved for the toe tag landing. Under reduced motion, movement becomes a short fade and the spring becomes ease-out.
